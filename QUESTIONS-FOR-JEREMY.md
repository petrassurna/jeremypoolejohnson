### 5. Rates

- What do you want to charge per lesson?
  A: $50 half an hour, $100 per hour

- Lesson length — 30, 45, 60 minutes? Different for kids and adults?
  A: Kids usually do half an hour, tenagers do 0.5-1 hour, adults do 1 hour

**Our recommendation:** publish your prices on the site. Most teachers hide them and end up fielding enquiries from people who were never going to book. Since your evening slots are limited, filtering matters more than volume.

**Also:** price _above_ the average local piano teacher, not level with them. You're a working jazz pianist — adults will pay for that, and they're far less price-sensitive than parents.

### 7. Former students who'd give a testimonial?

**Why it matters:** honestly, this is the most valuable thing on the list. An adult student saying _"I was terrified and it was fine"_ will convince more people than anything we can write. Google reviews are also the main reason people find you at all.

## What happens next

1. You answer these
